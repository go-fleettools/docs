# .github {#dotgithub}

Shared community health files, Renovate runner and preset for the go-fleettools
organisation. [Repository →](https://github.com/go-fleettools/.github)

**It has no tag**, and no release: it is configuration, and its README is two lines
long (a title and the sentence above). GitHub applies the community health files of a
`.github` repository to the organisation's repositories that have none of their own.
There is nothing to install.
